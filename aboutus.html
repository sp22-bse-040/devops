About Weather Now

Weather Now is your trusted source for real-time weather insights, forecasts, and personalized updates. We are committed to delivering accurate and beautifully presented weather data through an intuitive user interface that keeps you informed — whether you're planning your day, a trip, or monitoring extreme conditions.

Founded in 2024 by weather enthusiasts and developers, our mission is to make weather information both accessible and delightful. We blend technology, design, and data science to help users stay one step ahead of the skies.

From local temperatures and wind speeds to live camera feeds and weekly forecasts, Weather Now is here to be your everyday climate companion — across devices, across borders.
